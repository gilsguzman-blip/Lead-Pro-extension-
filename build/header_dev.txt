On one line, [^\n]* runs greedily to end-of-line and takes the MESSAGE BODY with it. Gerra's entry reduced to the literal string "inbound text message" -- the word STOP was deleted before anything tested it. _freshStopOnThisLead (v9.7.294, the Wendy Browne guard) has therefore been DEAD for every single-line inbound note, which is all of them.
